Refinement, October 15, 2026

The crop specimens were redrawn as original SVGs (200 by 320 view boxes) and arranged in a single horizontal row. The following botanical characters were checked:
- Pearl millet: elongated, dense cylindrical terminal inflorescence; fine bristles; linear-lanceolate grass blades attached at sheathing bases; nodal culm.
- Sunflower: yellow ray florets around disk florets; broad cordate/ovate toothed leaves with petioles.
- Canola, illustrated as Brassica napus: green terminal buds above open four-petaled yellow flowers; narrow siliques below; glaucous lobed lower leaves and smaller clasping upper leaves.
- Grain sorghum: compact branched panicle carrying rounded grains; long grass blades and sheathing bases. Compactness and grain color vary among cultivars; the drawing represents a typical compact grain type.
