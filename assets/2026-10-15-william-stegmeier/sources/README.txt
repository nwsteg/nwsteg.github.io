WILLIAM D. STEGMEIER: PRESERVED SOURCES
Saved October 15, 2026 for The Life and Research of Agronomist William D. Stegmeier
https://nwsteg.github.io/2026/10/15/william-d-stegmeier.html

The research folder preserves the original family research collection, including the memorial, research papers, institutional histories, photographs, bibliography and missing-document list. Files marked EXCERPT are convenience extracts; a PARTIAL file is not a complete article. The record folder adds the 1940 childhood census, 1950 family research leads, North Dakota veterans register, thirteen matching Marine personnel pages and one adjacent unit heading. A 1954 reserve listing is not evidence of residence in Chicago. The 1950 lead does not establish Bill's own residence that year.

The botany folder adds government plant guides and a Kansas State guide supporting the original crop illustrations. The Canadian government biology document is a self-contained text snapshot with simplified formatting; the other guides are unchanged source PDFs. The figures folder preserves the original editable SVG drawings and life map. The map-data folder contains the original Census boundary ZIP used to draw the map.

Sources copied from the original archive and the later downloaded record images are preserved byte for byte. Source attribution and existing notices are retained. Copyrights remain with the respective authors and publishers; this collection does not grant additional reuse rights. Source URLs, context, file sizes and SHA-256 checksums are in source-inventory.csv and source-inventory.json. SHA256SUMS.txt covers the individual files and inventories. The combined ZIP contains this directory's files except the ZIP itself. No account credentials, private correspondence, or draft record requests are included.

To refresh the inventories and combined ZIP after an intentional source addition, run scripts/build_stegmeier_source_archive.py from the website repository. This does not download anything or modify the source documents.
