# William D. “Bill” Stegmeier Research Archive

A family-history collection for Nicholas Stegmeier, assembled October 15, 2026. This archive preserves publicly accessible original publications about and by William D. Stegmeier (1931–1998), Kansas State University agronomist and breeder at Garden City and Hays. It is a substantial available-source collection, not a claim that every publication has been recovered.

## Start here

1. **1998 memorial by C. Tom Hash:** the two-page convenience excerpt in 01_Biography_Memorials_and_Photographs includes the photograph captioned “Bill Stegmeier and ‘Pearl’, ” colleagues’ recollections and family memories. The entire 176-page newsletter is preserved beside it.
2. **Hays history:** William M. Phillips’s 2001 history discusses Bill on printed 54–56 and contains an extensive research bibliography. Both the specifically requested SB663 B distribution and the complete 134-page institutional edition are retained. The complete edition includes the material absent from SB663 B; their overlap is intentional.
3. **Pearl Millet, Then and Now:** Spring 2017 AgReport, printed 11, includes a field-day photograph and Richard Vanderlip’s recollections. The complete magazine and a one-page excerpt are preserved.
4. **Original research:** the 1984 millet bulletin, 1993 and 1995 hybrid yield reports, 842 A/B and 843 A/B line descriptions, sunflower and plant-disease papers, and canola reports show his work directly.
5. **Scientific legacy:** government-archived projects document later experimental use of his breeding lines. These are classified separately from works he authored.

## Organization and index

The numbered folders follow the requested subject structure.00_Index_and_Bibliography contains Bibliography.csv, a readable Bibliography.md, Missing_Documents.md, a request checklist, and SHA256 SUMS.txt. Each bibliographic entry records the source and download URLs, role, relevant pages, retrieval status and local path. Blank or explicitly unverified information has not been invented. Author initials are retained when full given names are not established. For whole reports, report-level editorial credits and relevant chapter authors are distinguished.

All downloaded PDFs were checked for PDF signatures, parseable pages and text streams, and hashed for byte-level deduplication. Representative important pages and scanned documents were rendered or visually checked. This is not an assertion that every historical OCR character is accurate. Some legacy PDFs emit benign malformed-form warnings while their original pages remain readable; the files have not been rewritten to hide those warnings.

## Original files, excerpts and photographs

Original PDFs have not been shortened or recompressed. Files marked EXCERPT are added conveniences extracted from an archived full original and are counted separately. Page-based excerpts can contain adjacent article text. A file marked PARTIAL is an incomplete source copy and is not counted as a recovered full article. Distinct source editions are retained; identical bytes are stored once.

Three original embeddedJPEGs are in 01_Biography_Memorials_and_Photographs/Original_Embedded_Photographs, with Photo_Provenance.md. They were extracted without AI enhancement or resampling. The 2001 and 2017 field-day photographs show the same scene in different published reproductions. Publication dates are not established photography dates. The original page context and captions are preserved in the PDFs.

## What remains missing

The most important unresolved originals include the 1998 INTSORMIL annual report dedication, several otherINTSORMIL annual reports and proceedings, some sunflower papers and historical performance reports, SRP782, the 1996 canola production guide, KS 3579 registration, and the fullPlainsman registration article. The availablePlainsman PDF contains only its first page and is clearly labeled partial. Consult Missing_Documents.md for the exact verified leads and reasons. The 1998 memorial itself and its photograph were recovered in full from the newsletter.

Public institutional archives sometimes return 404, 403 or 502 responses, and one legacy report host has a certificate-name mismatch. No paywall, login, certificate warning, or access restriction was bypassed. Error pages were excluded.
